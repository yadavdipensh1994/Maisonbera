Maison Béra

Since 1974, Maison Béra has been the quiet standard for the serious diner — whole tenderloins cut with ceremony, prime rib served with weight, and an atmosphere that rewards those who understand the value of time well spent.

Discover Our Menu

More than fifty years of tradition.

For fifty years, Maison Béra has been a private address for those who know that true dining is a craft — not a transaction. The room remains intimate by design, the service deliberate, and the heritage unmistakable.

The beef is treated with the reverence it demands. Portions are substantial because they are meant to be shared. The atmosphere — polished wood, amber light, leather — speaks of a tradition that does not apologize for its age.

"Heritage. Craft. Beef. Hospitality."
Maison Béra — Brand Promise
